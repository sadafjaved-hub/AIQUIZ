Energy transfers

Energy is what makes things happen. It can’t be seen, but we can see what it does: lights glow, cars move and toasters get hot. Let’s find out how energy moves from place to place.

Big idea: Energy is never made or used up. It is transferred (moved) from one store or form to another.

Forms of energy

💡

Light

Lets us see. From the Sun, lamps and torches.

🔊

Sound

Made by vibrations. Travels as waves.

🔥

Heat

Makes things warmer. Also called thermal energy.

🏃

Kinetic

Movement energy. Anything that moves has it.

🔌

Electrical

Energy carried by electric current in wires.

🍎

Chemical

Stored in food, fuel and batteries.

Useful and wasted energy

Most devices transfer some energy into forms we don’t want. This is wasted energy, and it usually spreads out as heat. A light bulb is for light, but it also gets hot to touch. The hot part is wasted.

Key words

Transfer

Source

Where the energy comes from, such as the Sun or a battery.

Wasted energy
